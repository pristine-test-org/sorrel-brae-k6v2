How Sorrel Brae works

Sorrel Brae keeps one shared list for the whole team, and every change shows up on the landing page as soon as it is saved.

The list shows the five most recent entries, newest first, and finished items carry a tick.

  • 1. Add an itemAnyone on the team writes a short title and saves it to the shared list.
  • 2. Tick it offFinished items are marked done and stay visible with a check mark.
  • 3. See it liveThe landing page reads the list directly, so nothing needs refreshing by hand.
